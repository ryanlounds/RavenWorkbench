Raven Workbench Instructions

Standard operating procedures for FRC Team 1310 CAD version control, locking, and production release.

1. File Explorer & Part Inspection

Navigate subsystem folders in Designs. Single-click any CAD file to inspect its metadata; double-click (or click Open) to launch the part PDM workspace.

2. Check-Out Locks & Check-In Revisions

Click Check Out before editing a CAD file to prevent conflicting binary edits. When finished, click Check In v+1 to upload an updated revision while archiving the previous version in Google Drive.

3. Release for Production (CNC / 3DPrint / ShopDrawing)

Click Release on a custom part to select its manufacturing category (CNC, 3DPrint, or ShopDrawing) and required quantity.

4. Manufacturing Files & Completion Tracking

In Ready for Production, upload STL files for CNC/3DPrint or PDF drawings for ShopDrawing, and mark parts complete as they are manufactured. Track overall progress on /dash.
